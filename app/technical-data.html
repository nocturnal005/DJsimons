The Five Levels of Framing

To ensure the longevity of artwork, we categorize our mountboards into five distinct technical tiers. These levels define the protection and archival stability offered by each material choice.

01

Museum Level

For high-value artwork. Uses 100% cotton fiber, providing 500+ years of archival stability. Entirely acid-free and lignin-free.

02

Conservation Level

Chemically purified wood pulp. Meets the strictest standards for collectable art. Archival for approximately 20 years in normal conditions.

03

Commended Level

Ideal for replaceable art. White core ensures a clean bevel edge that will not yellow over time.
